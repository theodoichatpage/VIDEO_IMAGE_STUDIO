VIDEO IMAGE STUDIO 0.3 - PHAM VI KIEM TRA

DA QUA KIEM TRA MO PHONG:
- Mau thumbnail va mau anh video duoc chon dung.
- Gui / dung / tiep tuc giu cung ma yeu cau, khong gui lai tu dong.
- Tien ich tai lai tab co the cho tiep ma cu trong sessionStorage, khong click gui lai.
- Phan hoi anh gan voi user-message co ma VIS_JOB, chi nhan anh sau yeu cau do.
- Ket qua lap lai khong tao them phien ban.
- Tao lai luu phien ban anh cu.
- Doi thu tu canh, ZIP danh so theo thu tu moi va giu dung lop.
- ZIP khong long archive; loai bo duong dan vuot thu muc.
- Xuat mot phan va ghi de can xac nhan.
- Nhap du an cu thanh ban sao, giu file cu, canh da gui khong tu gui lai.
- Nhap anh thu cong; loi bo tach khong lam mat anh.
- Ma ghep sai bi tu choi.
- Cu phap Python va JavaScript da duoc kiem tra.

CHUA KIEM CHUNG:
- Dang nhap va thao tac tao anh that tren tai khoan ChatGPT cua nguoi dung.
- DOM cua giao dien ChatGPT thuc te va quyen tai anh tu may chu anh.
- Cai dat, native folder picker va updater PowerShell tren Windows.
- Render bo cuc trong trinh duyet thuc: moi truong xay dung khong tai duoc Chromium.
- Chay suy luan SAM2 trong bo tach tren may nguoi dung.
